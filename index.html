Design notes — v4

  • Font: DM Serif Display is back for every heading (name, work card title, this heading) — same as the live site, paired with IBM Plex Mono for the technical/UI labels.
  • Continuous scroll: the playhead is now fixed dead-center and never moves. The ruler and every track's waveform scroll right-to-left underneath it on a loop, in perfect sync — this is exactly how Pro Tools' "Continuous Scroll" playback mode looks, rather than a moving playhead over a static timeline.
  • Dropped the per-clip filename labels and bounded clip boxes — with audio now flowing continuously past a fixed point, a single bounded "clip" no longer made sense. Tracks now just show raw scrolling waveform in their own color, closer to what continuous playback actually looks like.
  • Speed/density of the scroll and tick spacing are easy to tune once the direction feels right.
